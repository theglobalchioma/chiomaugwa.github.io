Signal / 01 — Product & Technical Communications

Dojah — AI, Identity & Risk Intelligence

Making a risk intelligence engine easy to trust

Dojah's products are inherently technical — identity verification, fraud detection and AI-powered risk scoring. My work turns those capabilities into messaging frameworks, product education, website copy, lifecycle email and sales material that answer one question fast: what is it, why does it matter, who is it for, and why should I care?

I developed the messaging and narrative framework for Profiled Risk, translating a complex, multi-part product — unified customer profiles, real-time risk scoring, no-code rules, case management, AI assistance and data-agnostic integration — into a story teams could understand in one read. I also write the monthly Dojah Digest, the newsletter that keeps fraud and compliance teams current on emerging threats, regulatory deadlines and product updates.

Afriwok — Service Marketplace

Turning a two-sided marketplace into content people trust

Afriwok needed to speak to two audiences at once — clients looking for reliable service providers, and professionals looking for consistent work. Instead of leaning on promotional posts, I built a content system around education, trust, engagement, conversion and social proof: explainers on how the platform works, provider-verification content, FAQs, success stories, referral pushes and feature launches.

I also wrote and directed the brand's core storytelling assets — the launch-day CEO and COO speeches, the bilingual (English and Pidgin) radio jingles, and the brand story video script built around two real user perspectives: a carpenter struggling to find clients, and a new Lagos resident struggling to find someone she could trust.

Signal / 03 — Integrated Campaign Planning

Afriwok — Lagos Market Penetration

Taking a digital product into the real world

I developed the go-to-market strategy that moved Afriwok beyond digital channels into physical awareness across Lagos — out-of-home ads, radio, tech-hub partnerships, estate activations, mall pop-ups, WhatsApp and referral loops, structured across an eight-week roadmap from awareness through conversion and optimization.

The centerpiece concept, Fix It Friday, was a weekly social and email series: an audience member submits a real household problem, Afriwok sends a verified provider to solve it, and the whole thing becomes content — product demonstration, storytelling and credibility in one weekly format.

Crystal Bricks MCS — Financial Services

Turning a business announcement into a customer story

Crystal Bricks needed to announce a new Lagos branch — a straightforward corporate update that risked reading like a press notice. I reframed the newsletter around what the expansion actually meant for the audience: closer access, shorter distance to financial solutions, and a real team ready to help.

The piece combined an announcement-led headline, location-based storytelling, supporting photography and a direct contact point — built to be read by a customer, not filed by a journalist.

Signal / 05 — Educational Content & Lead Generation

MIDA Digitals — Investor-Ready MVP Studio

Making investor-readiness easy to self-assess

Early-stage founders needed a way to know whether their MVP could survive an investor conversation — a subject that's easy to make sound intimidating. I built the content strategy around one direct question: "Is your MVP actually investor-ready?", and broke it into a practical checklist across problem clarity, core solution, target audience, prototype, traction, investor assets and founder clarity.

I also wrote the landing page positioning the checklist as a genuinely useful resource first, and a lead generator second — helping founders stress-test their idea against the questions investors are already asking, with a clear next step into a discovery call for those who needed more support.

Lead magnet strategy Landing page copy Founder-facing content Go-to-market messaging

My approach

How I think about communication

Four principles that carry across a fraud-detection API and a carpenter's referral problem alike.

01

Technical products

I translate complex product and AI concepts into narratives a non-technical reader can follow on the first pass — without flattening what makes the product actually work.

02

Research & information

I look for the story, context and audience relevance sitting underneath the data — the version of the information that a reader will actually retain.

03

Content systems

I build content around what an audience needs to understand, not simply what a brand wants to announce — education and trust before conversion.

04

Campaigns

I connect messaging, channel choice, audience behaviour and a measurable action — so a campaign is a system, not a series of unrelated posts.
